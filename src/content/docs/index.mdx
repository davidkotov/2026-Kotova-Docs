Kotova X compares live quotes from several independent swap providers and routes
your order to whichever one offers the most for your trade. Your funds go directly
from your wallet to the provider that executes the swap. They never pass through
Kotova.

These pages explain what that means in practice, what happens at each step of a
swap, and what to do when something does not go to plan.

## What Kotova is

Kotova is a comparison and routing layer, not an exchange. It does not hold your
funds, it does not trade against your order, and it does not require an account.

That distinction shapes almost everything else in this documentation, so it is worth
being precise about it up front:

- **Kotova compares** — for every swap it asks several liquidity providers what they
  would give you, in parallel.
- **Kotova routes** — it sends your order to the one offering the best result, unless
  you have chosen otherwise.
- **The provider executes** — you send your funds to a deposit address belonging to
  that provider, and they send the swapped asset to your wallet.

Kotova is not a party to the transfer at any point. There is no stage at which your
assets sit in a Kotova wallet, not even briefly.

## The three products

Only one of the three is available today. The other two are on the roadmap and
cannot currently be used.

| Product | What it does | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kotova X** | Non-custodial instant swaps between crypto assets | **Live** |
| **Kotova Pay** | Crypto to SEPA payments and invoice handling | Planned for 2027 |
| **Kotova Deal** | Non-custodial escrow using multi-signature release | Planned for 2027 |

Everything in this documentation refers to Kotova X unless a page says otherwise.

## Something went wrong with a swap

Go straight to [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting). It covers the common cases —
a swap taking longer than expected, a lost order link, a missing memo, the wrong
network, an amount that differs from the estimate.

If your order needs a decision from you, your order page will say so. Swaps are
irreversible once broadcast to a blockchain, so it is worth reading
[Refunds and emergencies](/how-it-works/refunds) before you send anything.
